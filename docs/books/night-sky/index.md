# The night sky

*A book for anyone who has looked up on a clear night and wondered what they were seeing. It starts with nothing but
your eyes: how they adapt to the dark, how to find your way around the sky, and how to tell a star from a planet. Then
it follows the sky as it turns through the night and the year, the Moon through its phases and eclipses, and the
planets, meteors and satellites that cross it. It ends on Mars, under the same stars. Along the way it gives the names
and stories that peoples all over the world have given to the sky.*

## How to read it

- **In order.** Each chapter uses the ones before it. You need no telescope until chapter 19, and no maths until the
  "Working it out" sections.
- **Every level.** Each chapter starts gently, for a first night out. The middle sections go further, "Working it
  out" does the numbers, and the last section is for readers who want the full story.
- **Go outside.** Most chapters give you something to look for on the next clear night.
- **Mars beside the Earth.** Wherever the numbers are compared, Mars comes first.
- **The history is in another book.** For the full story of how each people read the sky, follow the links to the
  [history of astronomy](../history/index.md).
