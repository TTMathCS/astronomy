# Astronomy, book by book

Each book follows one part of astronomy from what you can see on a clear night to the questions astronomers are
still working on. Read a book in order: every chapter needs only the ones before it. The physics behind the sky
(gravity, light, heat, radiation) is taught step by step on [physics.akadian.ca](https://physics.akadian.ca/), and
the books link to those lessons instead of repeating them.

## How a chapter works

- **It opens with a box** saying what you will learn, which chapters you need first, and which sections are
  beginner, intermediate, advanced or expert. Skip the harder sections on a first read and come back later.
- **Every idea comes with its reason**: how it was measured, and by whom.
- **Worked examples** show every step.
- **Check yourself** ends each chapter: questions from easy to hard, each with a full answer folded under it. Try
  first, then open it.
- **Sources** sit in a closed box at the end of each section. Open it to see where a fact comes from.
