# Light and telescopes

*Almost everything we know about the sky has come to us as light. This book is about that light and the instruments
that catch it. It starts with what light is, how fast it goes and what its colours mean, then follows it through
mirrors and lenses into telescopes, cameras and spectrographs. It goes on to the light our eyes cannot see, from radio
waves to gamma rays, and to the telescopes that fly above the air. It ends at Mars, with the cameras that have looked
at it from orbit and the lasers that read its rocks.*

## How to read it

- **In order.** Each chapter uses the ones before it. The first book, [The night sky](../night-sky/index.md), is a good
  start, but it is not needed.
- **Every level.** Each chapter starts gently. The middle sections go further, "Working it out" does the numbers, and
  the last section is for readers who want the full story.
- **The physics is on another site.** Where an idea is taught step by step on
  [physics.akadian.ca](https://physics.akadian.ca/), the chapter links to the lesson instead of repeating it.
- **Mars beside the Earth.** Wherever the numbers are compared, Mars comes first.
- **The history is in another book.** For the full story of who found what, follow the links to the
  [history of astronomy](../history/index.md).
